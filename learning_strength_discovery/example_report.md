## Your trial choices

This trial is also fictional. The choices follow Sam's review.

- Daily time: You choose up to 20 minutes because that is time you have available. This is not measured concentration.
- Focus and breaks: Try one small task, pause when wanted, then decide whether to return within your chosen window.
- Encouragement: Private comments about your work, without points.
- Support: A visible goal, an optional first-step check, and hints you can request.
- Challenge: Start independently with help available. Check subject readiness separately before choosing lesson difficulty.

Review after the next session. Ask what helped and check learning with a fresh attempt without the same help. Change the arrangement sooner if wanted.
