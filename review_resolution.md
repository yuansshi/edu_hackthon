## A1 / S7-D-01 — clear findings after improvement

**User decision:** after a child improves, the finding can clear.

The revised concept makes the teacher card show current teaching needs. For this
draft, improvement is demonstrated when the child applies the targeted concept
on a fresh unaided problem with supporting work meeting reviewed lesson criteria.
An answer corrected with help leads to that independent check. No fixed streak or
universal number of correct answers has been added.

Independent improvement clears the active finding and its count, while preserving
the earlier work and keeping later review due. A later confirmed difficulty can
reopen it. Unclear or missing work is shown separately. Teacher correction retracts
an unsupported interpretation while retaining the correction history; it does not
pretend the child improved from a difficulty they never demonstrated.
Unclear new work cannot clear a previously supported finding or create a new
confirmed misconception.

The brief includes the active-count example: three of six assessed learners need
practice; one improves independently; the active count becomes two of six.

## A2 / S7-D-02 — ask for more reasoning when evidence conflicts

**User decision:** ask the child to explain more about their thoughts so the model
can evaluate the reasoning and help the child reason through the task.

The revised concept asks one neutral, task-specific question at a time and allows
pointing, object actions, or speech. It compares the follow-up with the child's
original work and reviewed lesson criteria. It distinguishes a clarified phrase
from a supported misconception and from a still-unresolved interpretation.

Until resolved, conflicting evidence stays outside confirmed-misconception counts.
If the interaction supplies teaching or hints, a fresh independent problem is
required before clearing an established finding. The model evaluates observable
reasoning; it does not claim to read thoughts or infer understanding from verbosity.
