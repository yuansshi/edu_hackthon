## What this proposes

ExplainBack Classroom is a local, two-tab Grade 2 math prototype covering addition and subtraction with regrouping. Learners submit frozen drawings or written work with spoken or written explanations, review recognized evidence, and receive an authored clarification, teaching response, or fresh independent check. The server controls learning decisions and teacher counts; a supported difficulty clears only after qualifying unaided work on a fresh problem. Drawings, transcripts, findings, and corrections remain in memory until reset or restart, while recorded audio is released after transcription.
