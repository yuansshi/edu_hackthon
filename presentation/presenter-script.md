# Smart Pants — three-minute presenter script

## Slide 1 · 0:00–0:25 · Who Are You

“We’re Smart Pants: Yuan Shi, Software Engineer, and Heather Bian, Product
Marketing. Heather builds Discovery and student profiles. Yuan builds tutoring,
reasoning and verification. Together, we’re connecting those two parts into one
learner journey.”

## Slide 2 · 0:25–1:05 · What Are You Solving

“A correct answer can hide a misunderstanding, and learners need different ways
into a lesson. We start by asking what helps the learner, let them review their
profile, and use their confirmed choices to shape teaching. Then we check the
reasoning on a fresh problem. Today’s preview uses text and visual math, with
optional device voice for Discovery prompts. These are synthetic examples.”
